reeldog

A personal video publishing tool by Elber Ribeiro

reeldog turns stories into short narrated videos and publishes them to accounts its operator owns on Instagram and TikTok.

How reeldog works

  • reeldog writes a script, generates illustrations and a voice-over, and renders a vertical video.
  • The operator reviews every video in a private chat and approves or rejects the video.
  • Approved videos go to the operator's own accounts through the official Instagram and TikTok APIs. On TikTok, reeldog uploads the video as a draft, and the operator finishes the post in the TikTok app.
  • Every video carries the platform's AI-generated label where the platform supports one.

Who uses reeldog

Only the operator. reeldog has no public sign-up and never posts to anyone else's account.
