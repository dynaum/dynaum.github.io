reeldog Privacy Policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

reeldog is a personal software tool run by Elber Ribeiro. reeldog publishes short videos to social media accounts owned by its operator. This page explains what data reeldog handles.

Who signs in

Only the operator signs in to reeldog with TikTok or Instagram. reeldog has no public sign-up, and viewers of the videos never sign in to reeldog.

Data reeldog stores

  • Access and refresh tokens for the operator's own accounts, kept in the macOS Keychain on the operator's computer.
  • Basic account details from TikTok and Instagram, such as the account ID, username and display name, used to confirm reeldog posts to the right account.
  • Post IDs, links and public performance numbers, such as views and likes, for videos reeldog published.

Data reeldog does not collect

reeldog does not collect data about viewers, followers or other TikTok or Instagram users. reeldog does not sell, rent or share data with anyone, and does not use data for advertising.

Where data goes

reeldog sends videos and captions to TikTok and Instagram through their official APIs, only to publish to the operator's accounts. All other data stays on the operator's computer.

Deleting data

The operator removes reeldog's access at any time from the TikTok or Instagram app settings, and deletes the stored tokens from the Keychain. Write to the address below with any privacy request.

Changes

This policy changes from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
